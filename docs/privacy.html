Privacy Policy

KALADOR collects the minimum data needed to publish videos: TikTok OAuth tokens (access and refresh) and the TikTok open ID.

1. Storage: tokens are stored in a local file (tiktok_token.json) on the owner's computer. They are never uploaded anywhere.

2. Use: tokens are used only to upload and publish the owner's own videos via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

3. Sharing: no data is shared, sold or sent to any third party or analytics service.

4. Deletion: deleting tiktok_token.json revokes local access; revoke anytime from TikTok Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
